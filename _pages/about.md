## Research focus

I am currently pursuing graduate study at the **Dalian Institute of Chemical Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences**. My work sits at the intersection of **microbial physiology**, **constraint-based modeling**, and **synthetic biology**, with particular interest in translating comparative genomics and metabolic network analysis into clearer biological explanations.

<div class="home-grid">
  <article class="home-card">
    <h3>Methanol yeast diversity</h3>
    <p>Comparative analysis of strain-level phenotypes, carbon-source utilization, and physiological variation.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="home-card">
    <h3>Genome-scale models</h3>
    <p>GEM reconstruction, FBA and pFBA, flux interpretation, and genotype-to-phenotype reasoning.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="home-card">
    <h3>Biological design</h3>
    <p>Using computational evidence to support microbial chassis evaluation and C1 resource utilization.</p>
  </article>
</div>

## Selected themes

<div class="home-card-grid">
  <article class="home-card">
    <h3>Phenotype-genotype mapping</h3>
    <p>I am interested in how copy-number variation, orthogroups, and reaction-level differences shape measurable growth behavior across related microbial strains.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="home-card">
    <h3>Model-informed interpretation</h3>
    <p>Rather than treating simulations as black boxes, I use metabolic models to organize experimental questions and explain biological divergence.</p>
  </article>
