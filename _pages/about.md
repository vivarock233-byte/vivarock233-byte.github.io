## Research focus

I am currently pursuing graduate study at the **Dalian Institute of Chemical Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences**. My work sits at the intersection of **microbial physiology**, **constraint-based modeling**, and **synthetic biology**, with particular interest in translating comparative genomics and metabolic network analysis into clearer biological explanations.

<div class="home-grid">
  <article class="home-card">
    <h3>Methanol yeast diversity</h3>
    <p>Comparative analysis of strain-level phenotypes, carbon-source utilization, and physiological variation.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="home-card">
    <h3>Genome-scale models</h3>
    <p>GEM reconstruction, FBA and pFBA, flux interpretation, and genotype-to-phenotype reasoning.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="home-card">
    <h3>Agent-based GEM correction</h3>
    <p>Developing agent-based workflows to identify, evaluate, and correct errors in genome-scale metabolic models.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="home-card">
    <h3>AI virtual cells</h3>
    <p>Interested in multimodal, multiscale models that represent cellular states and predict responses to perturbations, with an emphasis on connecting data-driven learning to mechanistic metabolic models.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="home-card">
    <h3>Biological design</h3>
    <p>Using computational evidence to support microbial chassis evaluation and C1 resource utilization.</p>
  </article>
</div>

## Selected themes

<div class="home-card-grid">
  <article class="home-card">
    <h3>Phenotype-genotype mapping</h3>
    <p>I am interested in how copy-number variation, orthogroups, and reaction-level differences shape measurable growth behavior across related microbial strains.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="home-card">
    <h3>Model-informed interpretation</h3>
    <p>Rather than treating simulations as black boxes, I use metabolic models to organize experimental questions and explain biological divergence.</p>
  </article>
</div>

## Selected Research Projects

<div class="home-card-grid">
  <article class="home-card">
    <p class="education-meta">Ongoing</p>
    <h3>Agent-based GEM Correction</h3>
    <p>Developing an evidence-aware agent workflow to identify candidate errors in genome-scale metabolic models, propose corrections, and evaluate them against biochemical evidence and model behavior.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="home-card">
    <p class="education-meta">Undergraduate Thesis</p>
    <h3>Comparative GEM Reconstruction and Phenotype Analysis of Methylotrophic Yeasts</h3>
    <p>Constructed draft, strain-specific GEMs for 16 representative methylotrophic yeasts with AlphaGEM and performed reference-guided gap filling for growth on methanol. Integrated FBA/pFBA flux comparisons, orthogroup and gene-copy-number analyses, and methanol-utilization and tolerance phenotypes to investigate the genomic and metabolic basis of strain-level diversity.</p>
  </article>
</div>
