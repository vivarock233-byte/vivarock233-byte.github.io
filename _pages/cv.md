Selected Research Projects
======
* **Agent-based GEM Correction** — Ongoing. Developing an evidence-aware agent workflow to identify candidate errors in genome-scale metabolic models, propose corrections, and evaluate them against biochemical evidence and model behavior.
* **Comparative GEM Reconstruction and Phenotype Analysis of Methylotrophic Yeasts** — Undergraduate thesis. Reconstructed draft, strain-specific GEMs for 16 representative methylotrophic yeasts using AlphaGEM and COBRApy; performed reference-guided gap filling for growth on methanol. Combined FBA/pFBA with OrthoFinder-based orthology, gene-copy-number analysis, and methanol-utilization and tolerance phenotypes to investigate the genomic and metabolic basis of strain-level diversity.
